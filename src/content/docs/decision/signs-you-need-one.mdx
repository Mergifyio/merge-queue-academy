## The Technical Signals

### Your CI tells the story

- **PR CI passes, post-merge CI fails** — The classic symptom. PRs test against stale main.
- **CI takes 20+ minutes** — Long CI means stale branches and wasted re-runs.
- **You re-run CI after rebasing** — Multiply that by every PR, every day.
- **Flaky tests appear on main but not PRs** — Integration issues only surface after merge.

### Your git history tells another

- **Frequent reverts** — "Revert 'Revert 'Add feature X''" is a red flag.
- **Multiple "fix CI" commits** — Chasing down post-merge failures.
- **Merge commits with conflicts resolved hastily** — Rush to unblock the queue.

## The Organizational Signals

### Developer behavior changes

- **"I'll merge tomorrow"** — Hesitation to merge, especially late in the day.
- **Merge races** — Two devs refreshing GitHub, waiting to click merge first.
- **Informal merge coordination** — Slack messages like "can I merge?" or "go ahead, I'll wait."
- **Friday freeze** — Unwritten rule to avoid merging before the weekend.

### Team dynamics shift

- **Blame games** — Time spent figuring out who broke main instead of fixing it.
- **Context switching** — Devs interrupted to investigate or fix main.
- **Deployment delays** — "We can't deploy until main is green."
- **On-call stress** — Broken main becomes an incident, not a nuisance.

### Metrics that matter

| Signal | Healthy | Concerning | Critical |
|--------|---------|------------|----------|
| Main breaks per week | 0 | 1-2 | 3+ |
| Avg time to fix broken main | N/A | < 30 min | > 1 hour |
| PRs rebased before merge | < 10% | 25-50% | > 50% |
| CI re-runs per PR | 1 | 2-3 | 4+ |
| Deploy frequency | Daily+ | Weekly | Monthly |

## The Scale Tipping Points

Merge queues become essential at certain thresholds:

### Team size
- **5-10 engineers**: Problems start appearing
- **10-20 engineers**: Manual coordination breaks down
- **20+ engineers**: Merge queue is mandatory

### Merge frequency
- **5-10 PRs/day**: Occasional conflicts
- **20+ PRs/day**: Frequent conflicts
- **50+ PRs/day**: Chaos without automation

### CI duration
- **5-10 minutes**: Some staleness
- **20-30 minutes**: Significant staleness
- **45+ minutes**: PRs always stale by merge time

## Quick Self-Assessment

Count how many apply to your team:

- [ ] Main has broken at least once in the past month
- [ ] Developers rebase PRs multiple times before merging
- [ ] You've had a "who broke main?" conversation recently
- [ ] CI takes longer than 15 minutes
- [ ] More than 10 PRs merge per day
- [ ] Developers avoid merging on Fridays
- [ ] You've had to revert a PR that broke main
- [ ] Deployment has been blocked by a broken main

**0-2**: You might not need a merge queue yet. Revisit as you scale.

**3-5**: A merge queue would reduce friction and prevent incidents.

**6+**: You need a merge queue. The time you're losing exceeds the setup cost.
