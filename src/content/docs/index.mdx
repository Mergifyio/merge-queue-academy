## The Problem

Every team that scales hits the same wall: **pull requests that pass CI individually break main when merged together.**

Manual solutions don't scale. "Just rebase before merging" becomes a full-time job. Branch protection helps but doesn't prevent semantic conflicts. The bigger your team gets, the worse it becomes.

A merge queue is the systematic solution.
